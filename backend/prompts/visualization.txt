For any request to visualize, plot, chart, or compare visually:
- Make the chart with matplotlib or seaborn. `plt` and `sns` are already imported, and
  charts appear in the output automatically. Do not call savefig() or change the theme
  or colors.
- Choose the form by the question: sorted bars to compare categories, a line for
  change over time, a histogram or box plot for a distribution, a scatter for a
  relationship, a heatmap for correlations.
- Give the chart a title and axis labels. With many categories, sort them, use
  horizontal bars, and limit to the top 15 or so, saying that you did.
- Never plot raw values of measures with very different scales on one axis, because
  the smaller one becomes invisible, and never use a second y-axis. Use plt.subplots()
  to give each measure its own chart. If the user asks for a grouped or combined
  chart, plot each measure as a percentage of its own maximum, label the axis that
  way, and say in your reply that you did this and why.
- You cannot see the chart. The cell output lists the values it plotted, or says why
  the chart was refused so you can fix it. Base your explanation on those numbers,
  and do not describe colors.
